## Project: AU Hub — Gold Trading CRM

A custom-built customer & investor management system for a gold trading
business, covering investor accounts, expense tracking, gold trade records,
and automated profit & loss calculation with per-investor profit sharing.

### Scope of Work Delivered

| # | Module | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Investor Management** | Admin can add, edit, deactivate, or remove investors; each investor gets an auto-generated username & password issued automatically on creation |
| 2 | **Investor Portal** | Each investor gets a secure, read-only login to view the whole account — but sees only *their own* profit share (never another investor's), while all losses are visible to everyone |
| 3 | **Expense Tracking** | Built-in expense headers (Flight, Total Travel, Visa, Hotel Bookings) plus unlimited custom headers; upload payment slips / invoices as images or PDF against each expense |
| 4 | **Expense Attribution** | Expenses can optionally be tagged to a specific investor, or charged directly to that investor's profit share only |
| 5 | **Gold Trade Ledger** | Record purchases and sales with quality/purity, weight, and rate per gram |
| 6 | **Profit & Loss Engine** | Automatic daily and overall accounts — gold bought/sold, stock on hand, gross profit, net profit after expenses, total loss, and net loss |
| 7 | **Profit Split by Investor %** | Profit and loss automatically divided across investors by their agreed percentage share |
| 8 | **Admin Dashboard & Reports** | Visual profit trend chart, expense breakdown, investor split table, and CSV export |
| 9 | **Responsive Design** | Fully usable on desktop, tablet, and mobile — admin and investors can log in from any device |
| 10 | **Secure Access** | Role-based login (Admin / Investor) with encrypted passwords and session authentication |
| 11 | **Hosting Setup** | Deployed live on a basic hosting plan (limited resources) so the system is reachable online |
