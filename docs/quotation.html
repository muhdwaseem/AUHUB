AU Hub — Gold Trading CRM

A custom-built system for managing investor accounts, expenses, gold trade records, and automated profit & loss with per-investor profit sharing.

SCOPE OF WORK DELIVERED
Investor Management
Add, edit, deactivate, or remove investors; each gets an auto-generated username & password on creation.
Investor Portal
Secure read-only login per investor — sees only their own profit share, never another investor's, while every loss stays visible to all.
Expense Tracking
Built-in headers (Flight, Travel, Visa, Hotel) plus unlimited custom headers; payment slips / invoices attach as image or PDF.
Expense Attribution
Any expense can be tagged to an investor, or charged directly against that investor's share alone.
Gold Trade Ledger
Purchases and sales recorded by quality/purity, weight, and rate per gram.
Profit & Loss Engine
Automatic daily and overall accounts — stock on hand, gross profit, net profit after expenses, total loss, net loss.
Profit Split by %
Profit and loss divided across investors automatically by their agreed percentage share.
Dashboard & Reports
Profit trend chart, expense breakdown, investor split table, CSV export.
Responsive & Secure
Works on desktop, tablet and phone; role-based login with encrypted passwords and session authentication.
